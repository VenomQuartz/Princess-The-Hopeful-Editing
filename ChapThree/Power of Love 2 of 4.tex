\subsection*{Bless}
\phantomsection
\label{subsec:Bless}
The Bless Charm enhances people, pushing their abilities to a peak and bringing their actions to perfection. Champions and Graces have affinity for it.

A Princess is perfectly capable of using Bless Charms upon herself, however bonuses from Bless Charms don't contribute to Charm activation rolls, or any roll to activate a supernatural power. Exceptions to this are noted in the individual Charm's text. 

\subsubsection*{We Rely on You (Bless \textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Instant, Presence + Empathy\\

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 1 Wisp\\

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} scene \\

A few words of encouragement can inspire anyone to succeed despite the odds. No person may be the target of We Rely on You while they currently benefit from a previous invocation of the Charm.

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}  \emph{Dramatic Failure:} The botched magic breaks the target's confidence in their abilities. Apply the Shaken Condition. 

        \emph{Failure:} Nothing happens. 

        \emph{Success:} Success grants the target the Floating Blessing Condition. It applies to all rolls except for invoking Charms (or anything equivalent) and the pool starts with one die per success. 

        \emph{Exceptional Success:} Extra Successes are their own reward.\end{myindentpar}
		
\noindent Each Invocation has an Upgrade to We Rely on You that strengthens the Blessing to two dice per success, but also limits the blessing to a narrower set of rolls. These upgrades are as follows: 

\begin{itemize}
\item    Sea-Foam's Touch (Acqua \textbullet) - Rolls using Mental Skills or Attributes.

\item    Wits of Wind (Aria \textbullet) - Rolls using Finesse Attributes.

\item    Engine Heart (Fuoco \textbullet) - Rolls using  Power Attributes.

\item    Steady Resolve (Legno \textbullet) - Rolls using  Resistance Attributes.

\item    Bejewelled Visage (Terra \textbullet) - Rolls using Social Skills or Attributes.

\item    Count No Cost (Tempesta \textbullet) - Any attack rolls.

\item    Burden of Rule (Lacrima \textbullet) - Any rolls made to protect an organization, group or person of the Princess' choosing. As a rule of thumb the roll should immediately prevent some clear form of harm like Damage or a Breaking Point. If the Princess names Alhambra this explicitly includes protecting the city's Wisp supply.

\item    Royal Glory (Specchio \textbullet) - Like the base Charm the bonus applies to all rolls while providing two dice per success, but a Princess can only use this upgrade upon herself. 

 \end{itemize}
 
 \begin{myindentpar}{10pt}
 
 \textbf{Upgrade: Beginner's Luck}
 
 Prerequisite: Sea-Foam's Touch or Bejeweled Visage
 
 When using either Sea-Foam's Touch or Bejeweled Visage any roll where a dice are added to a Skill ignores the Unskilled Penalty. 
 
 \end{myindentpar}
  
\subsubsection*{What's That Sound (Bless \textbullet, Aria \textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Instant

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 1 Wisp

\noindent \textbf{Dice Pool:} Wits + Composure

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} 1 scene \\

\noindent With a breath of excited air the Princess sends her target into a state of extraordinary alertness. The target gains the condition Alert. 

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}\textbf{Upgrade: Collective}

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} +1 Wisp

The Princess may use the Charm on a social group that can see or hear her, using the Commonalty modifier. 
\end{myindentpar}

 
\subsubsection*{I'm the Best! (Bless \textbullet, Specchio \textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Reflexive and Resisted, Presence + Wits - Composure 

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 1 Wisp

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} 1 extended action\\

\noindent Others might say that they are the masters of their fields. But, simply, the Enlightened of the Queen of Mirrors know this to be false. They can’t be better. This Charm can be used to enhance any contested action against an opponent who has a higher dice pool than they do.

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt} \emph{Dramatic Failure:} The Noble refuses to accept that she is the less skilled and covers her defeat with bluster and bravado. She takes the Narcissism Condition.

\emph{Failure:} Nothing Happens 

\emph{Success:}  Each success removes one dice from the target’s pool, and adds one dice to the Noble’s pool. The number of dice transferred is capped by the Specchio rating of the Noble; such glorious victory comes from one’s own strength, after all. This may not reduce the target’s pool to less than zero.

\emph{Exceptional Success:}  The Noble makes her victory look easy. She gains an exceptional success in the contest with 3 successes instead of 5.

\textbf{Upgrade: Triumphantly} (Specchio 4)

The limitation that the target must have a higher initial dice pool than the Noble is now waived. In addition, if the foe is reduced to a dice pool of zero, the Noble may reflexively spend a Willpower point to force a Dramatic Failure.\end{myindentpar}

\subsubsection*{Intuitive Flash (Bless \textbullet\textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Instant, Intelligence + Academics

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 1 Wisp

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} 1 scene \\

\noindent The Princess opens new pathways in the target's spirit, allowing them to draw deeper from the well of their experience and expertise. 

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt} \emph{Dramatic Failure:} The Noble seals off pathways, the target takes the Cursed Condition and loses a random Speciality for a scene.

\emph{Failure:} Nothing Happens 

\emph{Success:} The target gains the Informed Condition as a Boon Condition (which means it does not grant Beats), the topic covered by the Condition is one of their Specialities. This does not create knowledge but enhances memories and allows the target to combine old information through magically enhanced intuition. After benefiting from Touch of Fortune the target cannot benefit from a second Invocation until at least once scene after they Resolve the Condition. 

\emph{Exceptional Success:} The Informed Condition is broader, the topic covers two Specialities.\end{myindentpar}

\subsubsection*{Touch of Fortune (Bless \textbullet\textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Reactive, Wits + Empathy

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 1 Wisp

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} Duration: 1 scene \\

\noindent The Princess grants a blessing of good fortune to a person she can see.

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}

\emph{Dramatic Failure:} The target loses her confidence. She gains the Shaken Condition

\emph{Failure:} Nothing Happens 

\emph{Success:}  The target gains the Fortunate Tilt, blessing a number of rolls up to the activation successes. A Noble may cast Touch of Fortune at the same moment another person acts to bless that action.

\emph{Exceptional Success:} The target gains the Fortunate Tilt for many rolls.

\textbf{Upgrade: Well-timed}

The target's good fortune comes when most needed. Before the target makes a roll, he declares whether the Charm will affect it. The number of rolls affected does not change. 

\textbf{Upgrade: Strong}

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} +1 Wisp

The Princess grants a stronger blessing; the rolls affected by the Charm gain the 8-again quality.

\textbf{Upgrade: Perfected}

Requires Strong

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} +3 Wisps

The blessed actions become deceptively easy; instead of gaining 9-again the rolls affected by the Charm gain the rote quality. Strong and Perfected cannot both be applied to the same activation.

\textbf{Upgrade: Touch of Misfortune (Lacrima \textbullet\textbullet)}

The target takes the Misfortunate Condition, they do not gain 10 Again and subtract any 1s from their Successes. Negative Successes become a Dramatic Failure. 

\end{myindentpar}

\subsubsection*{Take Backsies (Bless \textbullet\textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Reactive, Wits + Empathy

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 2 Wisp

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} Duration: Lasting \\

Spotting a friend on the verge of trouble, a Princess bestows the gift of a second chance. This Charm may only be invoked immediately after another dice-roll. 

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}

\emph{Dramatic Failure:} If the original roll was a Success it's downgraded to a Failure. If it was a Failure it is downgraded to a Dramatic Failure. (The Storyteller should veto any attempt to penalize a foe with this effect.)

\emph{Failure:} Nothing Happens 

\emph{Success:} The target immediately re-rolls their last roll, the better of the two results stands. 

\emph{Exceptional Success:} The second roll also benefits from 9again, or 8again if the target already has 9again.

\textbf{Upgrade: No Chance (Lacrima \textbullet\textbullet)}

The Princess invokes this charm upon her enemy. The worse result of the two rolls stands. 

\end{myindentpar}

\subsubsection*{Tranquil Glade (Bless \textbullet\textbullet, Legno \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Reactive, Composure + Empathy

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 1 Wisp

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} Duration: Scene \\

By enveloping a friend in the calm of a peaceful forest glade the Princess protects him from mind or emotion affecting supernatural abilities. 

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}

\emph{Dramatic Failure:} The Princess takes a psychic backlash and cannot add Inner Light to rolls to protect herself from mind or emotion affecting abilities. 

\emph{Failure:} Nothing Happens 

\emph{Success:} The target gains mental shield equivalent to the defensive effects of Inner Light or similar supernatural advantages. Each Success is equivalent to one dot of Inner Light. This shield cannot stack with Inner Light or other Supernatural Advantages, use the higher defense.

\emph{Exceptional Success:} Extra successes are their own reward. 

\textbf{Upgrade: Forest Bastion (Legno \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet\textbullet)}

\textbf{Cost:} +1 Wisp, +1 Willpower

The Princess may apply Commonality to the roll to protect multitudes.

\end{myindentpar}

\subsubsection*{Charms of Enhancement (Bless \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet, Invocation \textbullet\textbullet)}

\emph{Action:} Instant \\ 

\emph{Dice pool:} Variable \\

\emph{Cost:} 2 Wisps\\

\emph{Duration:} 1 scene\\

\noindent Charms of Enhancement are a set of Charms that provide the Blessing Condition adding Activation Successes, up to a maximum of the relevant Invocation, in dots to one of the target's Attributes for a scene. A character cannot benefit from two invocations of the same Charm of Enhancement at the same time. She may benefit from multiple different Charms of Enhancement providing they affect different Attributes (Regiis Gloria is an exception and can stack with any other Charm of Enhancement). Charms of Enhancement cannot increase any Attribute above the (Princess' maximum Transformed Attribute rating + 5). The Charms are: 

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Drawn by the Moon (Acqua \textbullet\textbullet):} Roll : Intelligence + Science to enhance a Mental Attribute.
\item \textbf{Wind-Borne Grace (Aria \textbullet\textbullet):} Roll Presence + Expression to enhance a Finesse Attribute. 
\item \textbf{Stoke the Furnace (Fuoco \textbullet\textbullet):} Roll Strength + Persuasion to enhance a Power Attribute.
\item \textbf{Forest Sanctuary (Legno \textbullet\textbullet):} Roll Stamina + Survival to enhance a Resistance Attribute.
\item \textbf{Crown Jewels (Terra \textbullet\textbullet):} Roll Manipulation + Persuasion to enhance a Social Attribute.
\item \textbf{Sic Semper Proditor (Lacrima \textbullet\textbullet):} Roll Presence + Intimidation to apply the Cursed Condition and reduce a Social Attribute. An Attribute cannot be reduced below one.
\item \textbf{Avenger’s Might (Tempesta \textbullet\textbullet):} Tempesta's Charm of Enchantment does not enhance an Attribute. Instead Roll  Resolve + Brawl to enhance every roll in combat. 
\item \textbf{Regiis Gloria (Specchio \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet):} Roll Presence + Expression and split the Successes among any Attribute. No attribute can be raised by more than Specchio dots. A Princess can only invoke Regiis Gloria upon herself, and she may stack the benefit with another Charm of Enhancement.  
\end{itemize}


\begin{myindentpar}{10pt} 

\textbf{Upgrade: Flexible (Invocation \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet)}

The Noble may add dots to all of the target’s Attributes within the Charm's purview. She distributes her successes across the Attributes; she still cannot add more than her Invocation to any one Attribute. This Upgrade must be purchased separately for each Charm of Enhancement and cannot be acquired for Regiis Gloria. 

\end{myindentpar}

\subsubsection*{Light’s Aegis (Bless \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet)}

\emph{Action:} Instant, Presence + Resolve

\emph{Cost:} 2 Wisps

\emph{Duration:} 1 scene \\

\noindent The Princess extends a portion of her mantle over another person, granting him the power to ward off injuries. The Charm may not be used on anyone currently affected by it or anyone who has been affected by it in the current scene. 

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}    \emph{Dramatic Failure:} The Princess mishandles her power and injures herself. She takes 1 resistant lethal damage.

\emph{Failure:} The target is unprotected by the Charm. 

\emph{Success:} The target has limited access to the Princess’ Holy Shield. He takes the Aegis Tilt: each activation success each creates one shield-point which is equivalent to one Wisp spent on Holy Shield: A shield-point can be spent to downgrade Aggravated to Lethal damage, downgrade Lethal damage to Bashing damage, or negate all but the first point of Bashing damage. The Aegis may spend the same number of shield-points per turn as the caster's Wisps per turn. The Tilt stacks with the innate Holy Shield if used on a Noble; both may be used against an attack. 

\emph{Exceptional Success:} Extra successes are their own reward.

\textbf{Upgrade: Queen's Guard (Lacrima \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} +1 Wisp\\

The Aegis remains dormant until it is needed. The Duration of the Charm extends to Indefinite, but once the first shield-point is used the Charm will fade at the end of the scene. 

 \end{myindentpar}

\emph{Failure:} The target is unprotected by the Charm. 

\emph{Success:} The target takes the Lucky Escape Tilt. If the their last health box is filled with Lethal or Aggravated damage they replace that box with bashing Damage. This happens at the start of the target's next turn. 

\emph{Exceptional Success:} The last two Health boxes are affected.

\textbf{Upgrade: Collective}

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} +1 Wisp \\

The Princess may use the Charm on a social group that can see her, using the Commonalty modifier. 

\end{myindentpar}

\subsubsection*{Jade Palm Strike (Bless \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet, Terra \textbullet\textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Instant, Presence + Expression

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} 1 scene \\

The Princess wraps a friend's hands in a magical aura that mimics jade and cuts the flesh of servants of Darkness.

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}    \emph{Dramatic Failure:} The target's arm turns into solid Jade. Take the Arm Wreck Tilt for the scene. 

    \emph{Failure:} For a moment, the target feels spiritually cleansed.

    \emph{Success:} The target acquires the mystical purity of jade. His flesh has the same effects on creatures of Darkness that jade does until the Charm ends and he gains a bonus die to resist Dark magic. Jade Palm Strike cannot stack bonuses with physical jade. 
    
    \emph{Exceptional Success:} The target also gains the Blessing Condition and gains +2 dice to resist any Breaking Point caused by the Darkness. 

\textbf{Upgrade: Armed}

The target's enchanted aura spreads to his weapons. A weapon in his hands acts on Dark creatures as if made of jade. The aura comes from the target, not the weapon, so it fades from a weapon when he releases it; however, it lingers long enough for a thrown object or a bullet to hit its target.

\subsubsection*{Tendril of Nothing (Bless \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet\textbullet, Lacrima \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Instant and resisted, Presence + Intimidation - target's Composure

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 2 Wisps, 1 Willpower, Compromise at Belief 4+ with a -3 modifier

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} 1 scene \\

\noindent The void can seep into every mind. Give in, it says. Do not resist. Give up, and let your sorrows drown you.

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}    \emph{Dramatic Failure:} The Princess lets the void only into your own mind. She suffers from the Depression condition, it cannot be Resolved but fades on the next full moon.

    \emph{Failure:} The power does not activate.

    \emph{Success:} The stillness of the void settles into the heart and mind of a foe. The target gains the Condition Cursed, the affected trait is a Resistance Attribute of the Princess' choice, which is decreased by the Princess' activation successes. This Charm cannot reduce an attribute to zero dots. 
    
    If the last box on the target's Health track fills with aggravated damage, the Charm ends and the Condition is removed. The target still falls unconsciousness and if the target's Stamina is cursed then any leftover damage is immediately applied to their restored health boxes. When the Charm does end, the target's Attributes return to their normal values; all restored Health boxes are empty.

    \emph{Exceptional Success:} Such a show of force terrifies the foe, showing them the foolishness of standing up to the Princess. In addition to the other effects, he suffers from the Depression Condition until the next time he sees the sun.\end{myindentpar}

\subsubsection*{Peaceable Kingdom (Bless \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet\textbullet\textbullet, Legno \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet\textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Instant, Intelligence + Animal Ken

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 3 Wisps, 1 Willpower

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} 1 scene \\

\noindent The Queen of Clubs' avatar is guardian and teacher of the beasts. The Princess undergoes a second transformation on activating the Charm, assuming traits that suggest a large and powerful animal \textendash\, typically a lion, though bear-like and eagle-like miens are known, and there are rumors of one Hopeful who evokes a dragon. The change always suggests a king or queen of beasts, and the peril and majesty of the natural world; any mortal seeing the Princess is somewhat shaken, and she gains +1 on all Intimidation rolls against them until the Charm ends.

However, the main effect of the Charm is on beasts who see it. Any natural animal that can perceive the Princess when she activates the Charm has its Intelligence raised by 1 dot, becoming sapient. Further, the animals affected gain instant fluency in her native language, and the Charm grants them the ability to speak it comprehensibly if they have any ability to vocalize at all. The Princess may use Social Skills on the animals as she would with mortals, and gains a bonus on such rolls equal to her activation successes. When the Charm ends, the animals return to their former non-sapient state. If the Princess use the Charm twice on the same animal, it remembers everything that happened during the first use.

Until the Charm ends, the Princess gains the Invocation Lock condition keyed to Legno.

\subsubsection*{Sheltered by Her Hand (Bless \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet\textbullet\textbullet, Lacrima \textbullet\textbullet\textbullet\textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Instant, Resolve + Empathy, modified by Commonalty

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 1 Wisp, 1 Willpower, Compromise at Belief 4+ with a -3 modifier

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} 1 scene \\

\noindent To preserve her city and her subjects is the first principle of the Queen of Tears, and those who incarnate her are able to guard their allies from all harm. A Princess uses this Charm to protect citizens of Alhambra (no other Commonality group may be used; Lacrima may not be magically linked to Alhambra, but the avatar of Alhambra's Lady certainly is).

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}    \emph{Dramatic Failure:} The Princess fails utterly to protect her allies, and is crushed. She loses a point of Willpower and cannot spend or regain Willpower for the rest of the scene. In other words, she gains the Depression Condition but automatically suffers it's full effects without needing to fail in her goals first. 

    \emph{Failure:} The Princess cannot protect her allies. The Charm fails.

    \emph{Success:} The Princess undergoes a second transformation that drains all the color from her body and Regalia. All members of the target group who can see this transformation feel a breath of cold pass over them, penetrating through all clothing for a moment. 
    
    Until the Charm ends, the transformation expands and substantially modifies the Princess' Holy Shield; creating the Sheltering Hand Tilt: When anyone affected takes damage the Princess automatically protects them by spending Wisps at her usual rate for Holy Shield. If she does not have sufficient Wisps she drains the land around her. At the end of each turn in which the Noble took Wisps from the area, the Storyteller rolls dice equal to the number of Wisps she took. The drain generates Taint in the area of Severity equal to the successes on this roll, and Size large enough to include all the people it protected.
    

    \emph{Exceptional Success:} The Princess touches the determination that has sustained the Queen of Tears in her mission. She also gains the Blessing Condition. The affected trait is Inner Strength rolls and the increase is 2. The Blessing ends when the Sheltering Hand Tilt does so.\end{myindentpar}

\noindent \textbf{Drawback:} The Queen of Tears perpetually mourns for the lost Kingdom, and the sacrifices that must be made to sustain its remnant; her avatar bears the burden of her grief, and so also gains the Queen's Grief Tilt. At the end of every turn where she protected someone with this Charm roll Resolve + Composure: 

   \begin{myindentpar}{10pt}   \emph{Dramatic Failure:} The princess collapses into a hysterical mess for the scene, and suffers all accumulated Depression.
    
    \emph{Failure:} The Princess takes a -1 penalty to all future Resolve + Composure rolls to maintain Sheltered by Her Hand for this scene. In addition she adds one day of Depression to her accumulated total. 

    \emph{Success:} The Princess takes a -1 penalty to all future Resolve + Composure rolls to maintain Sheltered by Her Hand for this scene.

    \emph{Exceptional Success:} Nothing happens. \end{myindentpar}

When the Charm ends, the Princess immediately falls into Depression for the number of days accumulated during this Charm. 
    
Until the Charm ends, the Princess gains the Invocation Lock condition keyed to Lacrima.

\subsection*{Connect}
\phantomsection
\label{subsec:Connect}
Connect Charms trace and use the connections between people, or make and break such connections. Only Graces have affinity for them.

\subsubsection*{Fair Welcome (Connect \textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Instant, Manipulation + Empathy
	
\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 1 Wisp

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} 1 scene \\

Every person is intimately connected to a culture by a thread woven from a lifetime of experience, and through that experience a person learns a thousand little ways to act in order to belong. With this Charm the Princess can temporally move her connection to wherever she is now; allowing her to fit in and make a good impression wherever she goes. 

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt} \emph{Dramatic Failure:} The Charm works in reverse, making the Princess naive and awestruck by new people. She applies the Fresh Impression condition to herself, increasing her impressions of anyone she hasn't already formed an opinion about by one step. 

    \emph{Failure:} Nothing happens. 

    \emph{Success:} Until the Charm ends she automatically applies the Fresh Impressions Condition to anyone she interacts with for the first time as well as anyone who has spoken to the Princess before but has no opinion about her. Essentially, if the phrase \textquotedblleft first impressions \textquotedblright\, fits then the Charm functions. The Condition increases the target's impression by one step beyond what they would naturally be. In addition so long as the Charm is action the Princess removes any penalties for lacking a Specialty to a social skill or wearing inappropriate clothing. 

    \emph{Exceptional Success:} The Princess also gains the Blessing Condition, she has a +1 to all social rolls with the affected people. 
    
    \textbf{Upgrade: Second Chances}

Instead of an all-encompassing aura the Princess may instead focus this Charm on one single person, even one who has met the Princess before. The activation roll is Contested by the target's Composure + Supernatural Advantage. 

    \textbf{Upgrade: Introductions}
    
    The Princess may invoke First Impressions on someone other than herself. 

\end{myindentpar}

\subsubsection*{Helping Hand (Connect \textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Instant

\noindent \textbf{Dice Pool:} Inner Light + Empathy

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 1 Wisp

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} Lasting\\

\noindent  If a thing is worth doing, it's worth doing together. When the Princess assists in an instant teamwork action, she may activate this Charm instead of rolling the relevant dice pool. Each success on the activation roll becomes a bonus die for the primary actor. In effect this Charm replaces the usual dice pool for a secondary actor with Inner Light + Empathy. In an extended teamwork action The Princess can use Helping Hand multiple times, once for each roll. Any bonuses or penalties on the teamwork dice pool also apply to the charm roll, and a dramatic failure penalizes the primary actor's roll as usual.

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}\textbf{Upgrade: Enduring}

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} +1 Willpower

When assisting in an extended teamwork action, all the Princess' rolls are Inner Light + Empathy instead of the relevant dice pool.

\textbf{Upgrade: Guided}

Do not apply penalties on the teamwork dice pool to the activation roll.

\textbf{Upgrade: Twinned}

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} +1 Wisp

When the Princess activates the Charm, she also rolls the relevant dice pool, and adds successes from both that roll and the Charm. The pools are separate; any bonuses and penalties apply to both. Twinned cannot be combined with Enduring. \end{myindentpar}


\subsubsection*{Laying Down The Line (Connect \textbullet)}

\noindent \textbf{Action:} Instant, Resolve + Streetwise

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} 1 Wisp

\noindent \textbf{Duration:} One scene (Consecrated)\\

\noindent A Noble is a creature of divides; between Light and Dark, Transformed and mundane, Hope and despair. Drawing a line on the ground, she separates one area from another, and so becomes aware of anyone that crosses that line. Before this Charm can be used, the Noble must first make some kind of line or divide to empower, such as a chalk line drawn on tarmac, a rope thrown down, or even putting up a picket fence around a house. This is a mundane activity, unrelated to the actual use of the Charm, and these divides can be linear, or enclose an area. It can also empower pre-existing divides, such as a doorway, or a river. These divides must either measure no more than (Inner Light x 20) yards in any direction, or be in an area affected by the Consecrated Condition.

\begin{myindentpar}{10pt}    \emph{Dramatic Failure:} The Princess's clumsy magic temporarily manages to scramble her memories. She gains the Lost Condition, she is no longer able to remember how place connects to place. 

    \emph{Failure:} The Charm fails to activate, with a slightly pathetic spluttering noise.

    \emph{Success:} The divide is empowered, gaining the Line in the Sand Condition. She nominates certain targets and instantly knows if they cross the line (and which line, if she has more than one active). The Princess can nominate everyone, a single individual, or she may nominate a social group by adding Commonality to her roll. By default the duration one scene. If used on a permanent natural divide, such as a doorway, fence or across a bridge, the duration is increased to one day. If used on Consecrated ground the duration is until the area is no longer Consecrated.

    \emph{Exceptional Success:} The magic flows easily, the divide feeling natural, and so the Princess is refunded the Wisp cost for the Charm.

    Suggested Modifiers: Naturally secure building (+ Safe Place). Exceptionally strong memories of love and safety, such as a childhood home or childhood holiday home (+5). Strong memories of love and safety, such as a long time friend's home (+3). The character does not have position of the owner of the land to do this (-2).

\textbf{Upgrade: Illuminated}

\noindent \textbf{Cost:} +1 Wisp

Reaching out, the Princess sketches the bounded area in her mind, painting the delineation in liquid light. She is not required to make a mundane divide, and so may encompass any area up to the limit as part of the activation Instant action, and the line may not be removed by the destruction of the mundane component. 

\textbf{Upgrade: Unnatural}

The Princess has additional options when deciding who the lines should target. She may now choose anyone Supernatural, the Light touched (Transformed Princesses, Sworn, Beacons, Shikigami), or Creatures of the Darkness. For the purpose of this Charm \textquotedblleft supernatural\textquotedblright\, has a minimum threshold of a Minor Template.

\textbf{Upgrade: Sheltered (Lacrima \textbullet)}

\begin{mybox}
 
 \subsubsection*{It's Magic} 
 
 Regardless of their form weapons created by Kensai, Javelot and Levinbolt are treated as magical damage. They are the emotions behind a Princess' willingness to hurt another, or to defend another, made manifest. 
  
 This means that a Princess' may create a sword of the purest silver, but it will not inflict Aggravated damage to a Werewolf (though the Werewolf might not know that), but contrawise it will not be stopped by that anti-silver force field the Werewolf brought off a Mad Scientist. 
 
 Invoked upgrades can change this rule, for example after applying the Fuoco Upgrade Burning a Princess' attack consists of both magic and fire. Apply the greater Defense against either magic or fire, but any damage that gets through is counted as the more effective of the two. 
\end{mybox}

 \subsubsection{Strength of Heart \textemdash\, An optional rule} 
 
 Most role playing games, including Chronicle of Darkness, take a realistic view on combat where characters bring a certain amount of resources \textemdash\, health, willpower, ammunition, \textemdash\, into a battle which gets used up as the fight progresses. In fiction most magical girls start a battle with a base level of magical power and get stronger over time as the stress of battle forces them to tap into reserves of emotional strength they never knew they had. This optional rule recreates that paradigm. 
  
  A Princess begins always starts a battle with a single effective dot of Inner Light which increases over the course of a battle to a maximum of the dots on her character sheet. She increases Inner Light by one at the end of a round where any of the following occurs: 
 
 \begin{itemize}
 \item She takes a point of Lethal or Aggravated damage (not counting points downgraded by Holy Shield or similar) or a Tilt.
 \item She witnesses an Noble or Sworn ally receive an injury that prevents her from continuing to fight.
 \item She witnesses a mortal ally who is a significant character in the story receive an injury that prevents him from continuing to fight.
 \item She witnesses a non-combatant face a significant risk of serious harm.
 \item She successfully regains Wisps through the Inner Strength mechanic. 
 \item A new significant threat, like a second wave of Darkspawn, enters play. 
 \item She makes a speech. With the Royal Tongue Merit this takes an Instant action. 
 \end{itemize}
 
 If the Princess has permanently active Charms with more Upgrades than she can have active at her current Inner Light she reflexively activates new Upgrades as her Inner Light increases. If she enters battle with a partially more than ten Wisps then she unlocks additional wisps as her Inner Light increases and her maximum Wisp pool increases. For example a Princess with Inner Light three and 11/12 Wisps will start at an effective Inner Light one and can access ten of those Wisps. When she takes a point of lethal damage her effective Inner Light rises by one and she unlocks the last Wisp. 
 
 Antagonist Princesses may be exempt from this rule, to ensure that a powerful opponent cannot be quickly and easily overwhelmed by weaker but more numerous player characters without ever reaching the heights of Inner Light that make her into a threat.  
 
 With a little thought it should be easy to adapt this mechanic to other risky situations, like rescuing people from a burning building. However you should not feel obligated to ensure it applies to all Callings equally, or even all fights.
